5. Choose **Construct Here**. The building should appear exactly where the hologram was.
6. Pick the **Super-Heavy Hangar**. Check that it is still smooth (no lag) and the shape is easy to read at 1:5 detail.
7. Pick the **Long Runway**. Check that the near end shows and the game stays smooth.
8. If you can, test on a Realm with a second player. They should not see your hologram.
