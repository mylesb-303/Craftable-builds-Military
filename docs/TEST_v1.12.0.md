# v1.12.0 in-game test checklist

1. **Left turn:** place a Taxiway Straight, then Taxiway 90° Left Turn → Snap to End. It should turn the opposite way to the existing 90° Turn with a continuous yellow line.
2. **Long straight:** snap two Long straights end to end, then a standard straight. There should be no gaps or steps.
3. **T-junction:** snap it on a taxiway. Use Snap Right Exit, then pick it again, then Snap Left Exit. Both exits should line up, and the curved lines should be smooth.
4. **Arresting gear:** use the sequence runway straight → arresting gear → runway straight. The cable should be flush, and you should be able to drive or taxi across it without bumping.
5. **Overrun:** snap it to the last runway piece. Check that there is a red end bar, the chevrons point toward the runway, and no snap button is offered after it.
6. **Landing aids:** build the localiser, glideslope and VOR. Check the warning lights at night. On the VOR, climb the stairs onto the disc.
7. **Revetments:** taxi an aircraft into each bay. There should be nothing in the wing path.
8. **B-52 dock:** reverse a B-52 tail-first through the door. The fin and tailplane should clear. Climb both stairs onto the platforms.
9. **MQ-9:** park the drone in the shelter, enter the GCS and sit in the operator seats.
10. Rotate each piece through all 4 facings, and check the build on Xbox and iPad.
