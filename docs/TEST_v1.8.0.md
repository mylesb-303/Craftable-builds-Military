# v1.8.0 in-game test

1. **Barracks:** build the Army Barracks.
   - Check that the bunks are real beds.
   - Sleep in a bottom bunk and a top bunk.
   - Open a footlocker chest.
2. **Super-Heavy Hangar:** build it.
   - Taxi a B-52 or B-2 straight in and back out. Check that nothing touches the wings.
   - Look up at the door header: the lights should be part of the beam, not blocks hanging below it.
3. **Large Aircraft Hangar:** taxi a C-130J and an A400M in and out.
4. **Helicopter Hangar:** fly a Chinook in through the door.
5. **Hardened shelter:** taxi an A-10 into a Hardened Fighter Shelter.
6. **Main Security Gate:** drive through both lanes, then climb the gatehouse ladder to the overwatch.
7. **Watch Tower:** climb the spiral stair to the cab.
8. **Night check:** at midnight, walk through the HQ, the rotunda and a pier. No mobs should spawn, and no light blocks should be floating.
9. **Piers:** place two Pier Straights end to end over water, then add a Pier Corner. Check that the decks line up.
10. **Previews:** open the preview for each Air Force hangar. They are larger now, so check that each one fits where you want it.
